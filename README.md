## Why Reproducibility matters in social research?
In social research, reproducibility is crucial for three reasons:
 * Stops Cheating: It prevents researchers from tweaking data or cherry-picking numbers just to get a flashy result.
 * Protects Public Policy: Governments use social studies to make laws, tax policies, and school rules. Flawed research can hurt millions of real people.
 * Builds Trust: Human behavior is messy. Sharing open data and code proves findings are real science, not just made-up stories.
